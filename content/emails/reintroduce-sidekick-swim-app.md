### What’s new

**Daily check-in**  
Log each practice in seconds. Connect smart wearables to automatically track distance, strokes, and heart rate.

**Distance over time**  
See your yardage build across weeks and months — and celebrate monthly and yearly milestones with the team.

**Timed swims & personal records**  
Record timed sets and race efforts. Sidekick keeps your PRs so every breakthrough is easy to spot and share.

**Photos & videos**  
Share meet memories, poolside moments, or technique tips with teammates — all in one place.

**Practice schedule**  
Know when the next workout is, plan ahead, and never miss a session.
